# User Data Deletion — Brain Puzzle PRO

If you want to request deletion of information associated with your Brain Puzzle PRO game profile, follow these steps:

1. Send an email to **Manothanu58@gmail.com**.
2. Use the subject line: **Brain Puzzle PRO — Data Deletion Request**.
3. Include your in-game player name and, if available, your game account ID.
4. State that you want your game data and associated profile photo deleted.

We will review your request and may contact you to verify that you own the account.

After verification, we will delete or arrange deletion of the information associated with your account that we control, including applicable game profile information, leaderboard data, and uploaded profile photos.

Some information may remain temporarily in backups or be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days.

For help, contact **Manothanu58@gmail.com**.
