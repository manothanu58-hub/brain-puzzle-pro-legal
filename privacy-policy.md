# Privacy Policy — Brain Puzzle PRO

**Last updated: November 10, 2026**

Brain Puzzle PRO ("the App") is a puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and stored when you use the App.

## Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, the App may handle:

- **Game profile information:** your chosen player name, avatar, profile photo, scores, levels, coins, stars, and achievements.
- **Profile photos:** if you choose to import a Facebook profile picture, the App may access your public profile picture for use in your game profile.
- **Game and challenge information:** leaderboard scores, challenge invitations, battle results, and readiness status.
- **Account information:** a Firebase account identifier used to support game features.

## How We Use Information

We use this information to:

- Display your game profile and leaderboard.
- Save game progress and achievements.
- Support challenges and multiplayer battles.
- Display a profile picture you choose to use.

## Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may process information:

- **Google Firebase:** for authentication and game data storage.
- **Cloudinary:** for storing profile photos uploaded to the App.
- **Meta / Facebook:** if you choose to import your Facebook profile picture.

These services process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

## Sharing

Your public game profile information, such as your player name, avatar, and leaderboard scores, may be visible to other players. We do not sell your personal information.

## Data Security and Retention

We use third-party services and reasonable measures to protect information. Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the App's features or until a valid deletion request is processed.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. Parents or guardians may contact us with questions or deletion requests.

## Your Choices and Data Deletion

You may choose whether to import a Facebook profile picture. You can request deletion of your game-related information by emailing us.
